WEEK 9 – FAST-FAILOVER CONTROLLED FAILURE TEST

Topology:
s1 primary path: s1-eth3 -> s3
s1 backup path:  s1-eth4 -> s2

Fast-Failover Group:
Group ID: 100
Type: FF
Primary bucket: watch_port s1-eth3, output s1-eth3
Backup bucket: watch_port s1-eth4, output s1-eth4

Test:
Continuous h1 -> h3 ping was started.
Primary link s1-s3 was brought down.
Group statistics were checked during the failure.

Baseline group statistics:
Primary bucket: 526 packets
Backup bucket: 112 packets
Total: 638 packets

Failure-test group statistics:
Primary bucket: 682 packets
Backup bucket: 525 packets
Total: 1207 packets

Change during the measured interval:
Primary bucket: +156 packets
Backup bucket: +413 packets
Total: +569 packets

Observation:
The backup bucket processed a substantial amount of traffic after the
primary s1-s3 link failure, demonstrating Fast-Failover backup-path use.
Continuous connectivity between h1 and h3 was maintained during the test.

Note:
Exact failover/recovery time was not measured, so no specific
millisecond recovery-time claim is made.
